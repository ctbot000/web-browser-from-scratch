Kitchen sink

Every layout feature Mote implements, on one page.

Inline formatting

Text flows into line boxes and wraps at the measured width. It can be bold, italic, both at once, monospaced, small, big, x2 or H2O, a link, struck through or highlighted — and entities such as © → … € & ½ resolve through the full HTML5 table.

Floats and shrink-to-fit

A floated box is taken out of the normal flow and the line boxes beside it are shortened to make room, which is what pushes this paragraph to the right of the square. When the float is taller than the text next to it, following content keeps flowing around it until the float's bottom edge is passed.

Inline-block

Shrink to fit

An inline-block is sized by its own content, then placed on a line like a very large word.

Vertical align

Its baseline is the baseline of its last line box, unless vertical-align says otherwise.

Wrapping

When the next one will not fit, the line breaks and it starts the row below.
